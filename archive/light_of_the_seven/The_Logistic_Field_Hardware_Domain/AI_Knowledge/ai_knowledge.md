# AI Knowledge

## Overview

AI Knowledge systems capture, represent, and reason with human expertise. This branch covers expert systems, knowledge representation, and inference mechanisms.

## Internal Map

- **Expert_Systems/**
  Rule-based systems that emulate human expertise

- **Inference_Engines/**
  Mechanisms for deriving conclusions from knowledge

- **Knowledge_Acquisition/**
  Methods for capturing expert knowledge

- **Knowledge_Engineering/**
  Designing and building knowledge-based systems

- **Knowledge_Representation/**
  Formalisms for encoding knowledge
