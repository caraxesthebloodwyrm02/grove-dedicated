## Prompt Modifiers

### Style Tags
Add these to any prompt for consistency:
- `cinematic film grain, anamorphic lens flare`
- `Harry Potter aesthetic, magical realism`
- `warm golden highlights, cool blue shadows`
- `1990s British boarding school atmosphere`
- `practical lighting, no harsh artificial look`

### Emotional Intensifiers
- **High emotion:** `intimate close-up, shallow depth of field, catch lights in eyes`
- **Action:** `dynamic camera movement, motion blur on edges, sharp subject`
- **Quiet moments:** `locked-off camera, slow subtle movement, ambient sound design`
- **Transformation:** `time-lapse elements, morphing transitions, before/after framing`

### Negative Prompts
```negative
cartoon, anime, oversaturated, plastic skin, uncanny valley, modern technology visible, contemporary clothing, harsh flash lighting, dutch angle without purpose, excessive lens flare, AI artifacts, extra fingers, distorted faces
```

## Technical Notes

- **Sora:** Works best with detailed environmental descriptions. Front-load the setting, then action.
- **Veo 3:** Responds well to emotional direction. Name the feeling explicitly.
- **Both:** Avoid prompt over 300 words. Be specific about ONE moment, not a sequence.
- **Consistency:** Reference "young woman with bushy brown curly hair, brown eyes, determined expression" in each prompt for character lock.
