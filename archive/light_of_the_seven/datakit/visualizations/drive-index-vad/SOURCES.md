## Research Methodology

### Search Strategy
- **Databases**: IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Frontiers, arXiv, Google Scholar
- **Keywords**: "VAD model", "valence arousal dominance", "affective computing", "dimensional emotion", "driver monitoring", "mental health", "education", "chatbot", "robotics"
- **Timeframe**: 2023-2025 (current applications)
- **Quality Filter**: Peer-reviewed publications, regulatory documents, validated industry reports

### Validation Criteria
1. **Published**: Peer-reviewed or regulatory authority
2. **Current**: Within 2-year window (2023-2025)
3. **Validated**: Empirical studies or real-world deployment
4. **Relevant**: Direct application of VAD/PAD models
