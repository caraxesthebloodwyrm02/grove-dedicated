# Founders' Archive (Canon-Only)

**Root Directory:** `full_datakit/visualizations/Hogwarts/Founders/`  
**Scope:** **Canon-only** reference index for the four Hogwarts Founders.  
**Canon Standard Used:**  
- **Primary canon:** the seven *Harry Potter* novels.  
- **Secondary canon (optional):** officially published Wizarding World / J.K. Rowling background writings *only when explicitly presented as canonical background*.  
**Excluded:** fan works, theories, roleplay, “Spellbook Chronicles” interpretations, invented artifacts/events, and any unverified biographical details.

## Directory Manifest (Folders)

### `Founders/Gryffindor/`
**Founder:** Godric Gryffindor  
**Canon anchors (books):**
- Hogwarts was founded by the four founders, including Gryffindor.  
- **Gryffindor’s sword** exists and is a key artifact in the series.  
  - Appears in *Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets* (used to kill the Basilisk).  
  - Returns in later books; central to Horcrux destruction in *Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows*.  
- The **Sorting Hat** is associated with the founders and sorting (ties to founder lore across the series).

**Recommended contents for this folder (canon-only):**
- `README.md` with: brief canon summary + citations to relevant books/chapters (no speculation).
- `artifacts.md` covering: Sword of Gryffindor, Sorting Hat (only what’s stated in the novels).

### `Founders/Slytherin/`
**Founder:** Salazar Slytherin  
**Canon anchors (books):**
- Hogwarts was founded by the four founders, including Slytherin.  
- Slytherin advocated restricting admission to “pure-blood” students, causing conflict and his departure (as presented in *Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets*).  
- The **Chamber of Secrets** was built by Slytherin and contains a **Basilisk**.
  - Opened in 1943 by Tom Riddle; Myrtle Warren is killed (*Chamber of Secrets* + Riddle backstory).  
  - Opened again in 1992–1993; Harry kills the Basilisk with the Sword of Gryffindor; diary Horcrux destroyed (*Chamber of Secrets*).  
- **Parseltongue** is the key access mechanism to the Chamber.
- **Slytherin’s Locket** is a significant artifact:
  - It is a Horcrux and is destroyed in *Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows*.  
- Voldemort’s descent from Slytherin through the **Gaunt** line is established in *Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince*.

**File pointer (canon-only):**
- `../../salazar_slytherin.md` should remain a canon-only reference (no third-party narrative, no invented wand/Patronus claims).

**Recommended contents for this folder (canon-only):**
- `README.md` with: brief canon summary + citations to relevant books/chapters.
- `chamber_of_secrets.md` with: strictly the events, access details, and outcomes described in canon.

## Canon Citation Pattern (Required)

When adding or editing files under `Founders/`, include a short citation block:

- **Book:** *(title)*  
- **Context:** 1–2 lines describing where the information appears (chapter name/number if you’re tracking it).  
- **Claim:** the exact canon fact being recorded.

Example (format only; do not treat as a complete citation):
- **Book:** *Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets*  
- **Context:** Chamber legend discussed; Basilisk confrontation  
- **Claim:** “Harry kills the Basilisk with the Sword of Gryffindor.”
