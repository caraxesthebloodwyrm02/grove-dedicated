# Salazar Slytherin (Canon-Only Reference)

This document is **canon-only**. It summarizes information supported by **primary canon** (the seven Harry Potter novels) and **secondary canon** (official J.K. Rowling/Wizarding World writings where explicitly published as canon background).  
Anything not supported by those sources is excluded.

---

## Canonical Identity

- **Name:** Salazar Slytherin  
- **Role:** One of the **four founders of Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry**.  
- **House Founded:** **Slytherin**.  
- **Notable Ability:** **Parseltongue** (ability to speak to snakes).  
- **Core Belief / Conflict:** Advocated admitting only students of “pure-blood” magical ancestry; this brought him into conflict with the other founders and ultimately led to his departure from Hogwarts.

---

## Founding of Hogwarts (What Canon Confirms)

From the books, the following points are directly supported:

- Hogwarts was founded by **Godric Gryffindor, Helga Hufflepuff, Rowena Ravenclaw, and Salazar Slytherin**.
- The founders **selected students** according to values and traits associated with each house.
- **Slytherin disagreed** with the other founders over whether Hogwarts should accept **Muggle-born** students.
- Slytherin ultimately **left the school** after the dispute.

## The Chamber of Secrets (Canon Facts)

### Construction and Purpose
- Salazar Slytherin **built a hidden chamber** within Hogwarts: the **Chamber of Secrets**.
- The Chamber housed a **Basilisk** that Slytherin intended as a weapon to purge the school of those he considered “unworthy” (explicitly tied to his hostility toward Muggle-born students).

### Access Control
- The entrance is hidden and can be opened only by a **Parselmouth**.
- The Chamber can be accessed through the **girls’ bathroom** containing the entrance to the plumbing system (discovered in *Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets*).

### What Happened in 1943
- **Tom Riddle** (later Lord Voldemort), as a Hogwarts student, **opened the Chamber**.
- The Basilisk killed **Myrtle Warren** (later Moaning Myrtle).
- Riddle framed **Rubeus Hagrid** and had him expelled.

### What Happened in 1992–1993
- The Chamber was opened again (via Tom Riddle’s diary Horcrux acting through **Ginny Weasley**).
- The Basilisk attacked students; victims were **petrified** (not killed) because they did not look directly into its eyes.
- **Harry Potter** killed the Basilisk using the **Sword of Gryffindor**.
- The diary was destroyed with a Basilisk fang.

## Slytherin’s Heir and Bloodline (Canon Facts)

- The legend of the “**Heir of Slytherin**” is central to the events of *Chamber of Secrets*.
- **Tom Riddle** is confirmed to be Slytherin’s heir through the **Gaunt family** line (*Half-Blood Prince*).

---

## Parseltongue (Canon Facts)

- Parseltongue is a rare magical ability that enables the speaker to communicate with snakes.
- It is strongly associated in wizarding culture with **Salazar Slytherin** and later with **Voldemort** and those connected to him.
- Harry Potter could speak Parseltongue due to his unintended connection to Voldemort’s soul (a key plot point revealed across the series).
