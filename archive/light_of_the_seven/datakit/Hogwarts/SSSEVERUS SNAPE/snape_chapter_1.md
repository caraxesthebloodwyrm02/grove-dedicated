# Severus Snape (Canon-Only Summary)
## Chapter 1 — Early Life, Hogwarts Years, and the Half-Blood Prince

**Scope:** Canon-only summary based on the seven *Harry Potter* novels.  
**Excluded by design:** third-party interpretations, fan theories, invented events/characters, and non-canon frameworks.

---

## 1) Canon Snapshot

Severus Snape is a Hogwarts professor (Potions Master, later Defence Against the Dark Arts professor, later Headmaster) whose role in the Second Wizarding War is defined by espionage, secrecy, and a long-standing promise tied to Lily Evans.

---

## 2) Family and Origins (What the novels support)

- Snape grew up in a troubled home in **Spinner’s End, Cokeworth**.
- His parents are **Tobias Snape** (a Muggle) and **Eileen Prince** (a witch), making Snape **half-blood**.
- Snape’s childhood environment is depicted as unhappy and marked by conflict.

**Canon anchors (books):**
- *Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows* (Prince’s Tale)

---

## 3) Friendship with Lily Evans

- As a child, Snape meets **Lily Evans** before Hogwarts and recognizes her magical ability.
- Snape introduces Lily to the wizarding world and Hogwarts, and they become close friends.
- Their friendship strains as Snape becomes increasingly associated with Slytherin students interested in Dark magic and anti–Muggle-born prejudice.

**Canon anchors (books):**
- *Deathly Hallows* (Prince’s Tale)

---

## 4) Hogwarts: Sorting, Rivals, and Social Context

- Snape is sorted into **Slytherin**; Lily is sorted into **Gryffindor**, creating a daily social separation.
- Snape has a long-running hostile relationship with **James Potter** and **Sirius Black**, rooted in repeated bullying and mutual antagonism during their school years.
- Snape’s resentment toward James Potter persists into adulthood and affects his behavior toward Harry.

**Canon anchors (books):**
- *Order of the Phoenix* (Pensieve memory: “Snape’s Worst Memory”)
- *Deathly Hallows* (Prince’s Tale)

---

## 5) “Snape’s Worst Memory” and the Break with Lily

- In Snape’s fifth year, James Potter publicly humiliates him near the lake.
- Lily intervenes; Snape, humiliated and furious, calls Lily a **“Mudblood.”**
- This moment becomes a decisive rupture: Lily ultimately ends their friendship.

**Canon anchors (books):**
- *Order of the Phoenix* (Pensieve memory)
- *Deathly Hallows* (Prince’s Tale)

---

## 6) The “Half-Blood Prince” (Textbook Evidence)

- Snape owns a Potions textbook later used by Harry in sixth year; it is heavily annotated with improved instructions and original jinxes/curses.
- The book is signed **“This book is the property of the Half-Blood Prince.”**
- Harry’s use of the notes boosts his performance in Potions, creating friction with Snape.
- The book includes **Sectumsempra**, labeled “for enemies,” which Harry uses on Draco Malfoy with near-fatal results.

**Canon anchors (books):**
- *Half-Blood Prince*

**What the novels support (limited list):**
- Snape is the Half-Blood Prince.
- He created (or at minimum authored) spells and notes in that textbook.
- Sectumsempra is dangerous and causes severe bleeding/wounds; Snape knows how to heal it.

---

## 7) Early Alignment with the Death Eaters (High-level canon)

- After Hogwarts, Snape becomes a **Death Eater**.
- He later defects and becomes a key agent for Dumbledore, but the trigger for that shift is tied to Lily Evans and Voldemort’s targeting of the Potters.

**Canon anchors (books):**
- *Deathly Hallows* (Prince’s Tale)
- *Half-Blood Prince* (context around Snape’s past and loyalties)
