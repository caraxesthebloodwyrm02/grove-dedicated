## Founder Profile: Godric Gryffindor

### Origins

Godric Gryffindor hailed from the wild moors of the West Country of England, a land of rugged beauty and fierce independence. Born into a time of conflict between magical folk and Muggles, Godric distinguished himself early as a warrior-wizard of unparalleled bravery.

Unlike many of his contemporaries who preferred to hide their abilities, Godric believed that magic was a gift meant to protect the innocent and stand against darkness—regardless of the personal cost.

### Notable Achievements

| Achievement | Significance |
|-------------|--------------|
| Defeated Ragnuk the First's army | Demonstrated combat prowess and strategic genius |
| Co-founded Hogwarts | Established formal magical education in Britain |
| Created the Sorting Hat | Enchanted his own hat with the founders' collective wisdom |
| Forged the Sword of Gryffindor | Goblin-made, appears to worthy Gryffindors in need |

### The Sword of Gryffindor

A blade of pure silver, inset with rubies the size of eggs, forged by goblins under Godric's commission. The sword possesses unique properties:

- **Imbibes that which strengthens it** — After Harry slew the Basilisk, the blade absorbed its venom
- **Appears to worthy Gryffindors** — Manifests from the Sorting Hat in moments of true need
- **Goblin-made** — Never requires cleaning; repels mundane dirt and rust

## Temporal Patronus Connection

Many Gryffindors produce powerful Patronuses anchored to moments of moral courage:

```python
# Example: Harry's Patronus configuration
TemporalPatronus(
    caster="Harry Potter",
    memory="Choosing to live beyond the war"
).cast("Stag")
 .anchor_to_past("Final duel with Voldemort")
 .anchor_to_future("Standing with Albus at the edge of time")
```

The Stag—James Potter's form—represents protection, nobility, and the courage to face death itself.
