# Slytherin House (Canon-Only)

This file is **canon-only**. It summarizes information supported by the seven *Harry Potter* novels.  
Non-canon material (fan works, speculation, “expanded” theories, and tool-generated lore) is intentionally excluded.

---

## House Overview (Canon)

- **House Name:** Slytherin
- **Founder:** Salazar Slytherin
- **House Colors:** Green and silver
- **House Symbol:** Serpent
- **Common Room Location:** Dungeons beneath Hogwarts (near the Black Lake)
- **Ghost:** The Bloody Baron

---

## Sorting Hat Perspective (Canon)

Slytherin House is repeatedly associated in the novels with:
- ambition
- resourcefulness
- determination
- a tendency toward self-preservation

The Sorting Hat’s songs and dialogue across the series reinforce that each House values different traits and that sorting is not purely hereditary or deterministic.

---

## Salazar Slytherin (Canon)

From the novels, Salazar Slytherin is established as:
- one of the four founders of Hogwarts
- a Parselmouth (able to speak to snakes)
- someone who opposed admitting Muggle-born students, which caused a dispute with the other founders and led to his departure from Hogwarts

Slytherin’s legacy is central to *Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets* due to the Chamber’s creation and the “Heir of Slytherin” legend.

---

## The Chamber of Secrets (Canon)

- The **Chamber of Secrets** was created by Salazar Slytherin.
- It housed a **Basilisk**.
- Access required a **Parselmouth**.
- The Chamber was opened in the past by Tom Riddle (as a student) and again in Harry Potter’s second year, leading to attacks on students and the revelation of the diary as a Horcrux-linked artifact.

---

## Key Canonical Artifacts Connected to Slytherin

### Slytherin’s Locket
- Exists in canon as a significant heirloom tied to Salazar Slytherin’s line.
- It becomes a Horcrux and is ultimately destroyed during the Second Wizarding War.

### The Basilisk (Chamber of Secrets)
- The Basilisk living in the Chamber is killed by Harry Potter using the Sword of Gryffindor.
