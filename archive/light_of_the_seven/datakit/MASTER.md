# 🧙 MASTER: J.K. Rowling Appreciation Token System

> **One-page guide to understand everything**

---

## 🎯 What Is This?

A **tribute system** that generates personalized appreciation content for J.K. Rowling and the Harry Potter universe. It combines data, storytelling, and interactive exploration.

---

## 📁 Core Files (The Canon)

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `data/jk_rowling_appreciation.json` | **Master data** - all content lives here |
| `scripts/appreciation_generator.py` | **Generator** - creates tributes & letters |
| `visualizations/interactive/rowling_tribute.py` | **Display** - interactive CLI tribute |

## 🏗️ Data Structure (JSON)

```
jk_rowling_appreciation.json
├── metadata          → name, version, theme
├── narrative_segments
│   ├── origins       → her journey (timeline)
│   ├── craft_elements→ world-building, themes
│   ├── global_ripple → stats & cultural impact
│   └── personal_resonance → why it matters
├── knowledge_blocks
│   ├── book_milestones (7 books)
│   ├── hogwarts_houses (4 houses)
│   └── key_characters (7 characters)
├── fun_facts         → 12 trivia items
├── challenges        → 5 interactive tasks
└── learning_modules  → 6 guided lessons
```
