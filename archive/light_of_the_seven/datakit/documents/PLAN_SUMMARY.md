## 💡 Key Insights Driving Design

From the conversation analysis, seven key insights inform all three plans:

1. **Semantic Richness**: Content can be tagged across emotional, literary, technical, and cultural dimensions
2. **Multi-Modal Learning**: Different people learn through narrative, visualization, generation, and challenge
3. **Layered Complexity**: Systems should support beginner to advanced engagement
4. **Context-Driven Design**: Generic architecture enables reuse for other tributes/topics
5. **Modular Architecture**: Clear separation of concerns enables testing and extension
6. **Interactive Learning**: Active engagement (exploration, generation, reflection) deepens understanding
7. **Semantic Variations**: Same facts viewed through different lenses reveal new perspectives
